Scope

Kitchen of Words

A small web app that turns Japanese vocabulary practice into a cozy recipe-building experience, where each word becomes an ingredient or step in preparing a traditional dish.

The Unique Kernel

The distinctive thing is that learning is not just memorizing words in isolation: every vocabulary item becomes part of building a real dish. The app feels like a tiny Japanese kitchen experience, where progress is visible as a meal comes together one ingredient at a time.

Who It's For

A beginner language learner who is passionate about Japanese culture, food, and thoughtful design. They want a study habit that feels calming and motivating, not like a generic productivity tool.

Learner
Study dish words
Build the recipe
Finished dish + confidence

The Core Loop

The user opens the app, chooses a dish or meal, studies a small set of Japanese words related to it, and marks what they know. Each learned word adds an ingredient, cooking step, or part of the final dish.

What “Working” Looks Like

The proof of concept is a simple app where the learner selects a dish, reviews a small set of vocabulary, and sees the dish gradually build as words are learned. The satisfying moment is when a few study actions visibly transform ingredients into a finished dish.

POC Boundary
  • Small set of dishes or meal categories
  • Japanese words connected to each dish
  • Mark words as known or reviewed
  • Visual progress as ingredients or steps are added
  • Short demoable loop in under a minute
Later
  • More dishes and vocabulary sets
  • Richer animations and polish
  • Custom dish creation or level progression
  • Saved progress and repetition tracking
Explicitly Cut
  • Full restaurant simulation or business management flow
  • Large Japanese-learning platform or broad product scope
  • Advanced algorithmic learning system
